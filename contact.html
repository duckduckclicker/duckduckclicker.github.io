3. How to Submit an Effective Bug Report

Help us fix issues faster! When reporting a bug or broken iframe, please include the following technical details in your message:

  • Specific Game URL: The exact link to the game page (e.g., duck-duck-clicker.github.io/action/tunnel-rush.html).
  • Device & Operating System: Desktop PC, Mac, Chromebook, Android tablet, iPhone, etc.
  • Web Browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, or Brave (along with version number if known).
  • Steps to Reproduce: What happened right before the error (e.g., "Screen went black on level 3 after pressing spacebar").
  • Browser Console Logs: Any red error codes visible in your browser's developer console (F12 -> Console).

4. Game Developer Submission Guidelines

Duck Duck Clicker is eager to support indie game creators worldwide. Before submitting your build, please verify that your title conforms to our platform standards:

Technical Specs

Pure HTML5/WebGL/WebAssembly build. Must support responsive scaling and run at 60 FPS on modest hardware. Mobile touch controls are strongly preferred.

Safety & Monetization

No pop-unders, cryptocurrency scripts, or deceptive download triggers. Clean, family-safe content suitable for schools and general audiences.

Send your demo link or ZIP bundle to dev@duck-duck-clicker.github.io with your game title, description, thumbnail (512x512 PNG), and developer attribution credentials.
